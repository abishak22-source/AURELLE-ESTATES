Design & specification

Made for Chennai's climate and everyday rhythms

Vastu-conscious plans, cross-ventilated rooms and shaded outdoor spaces suit Chennai's warm days and seasonal monsoon. Coastal homes are detailed with the local climate in mind.

Structure
RCC frame for Seismic Zone III, with corrosion-resistant steel on coastal sites.
Flooring
Italian marble in living and dining, engineered wood in the master bedroom.
Windows
Aluminium sliding windows with toughened glass and mosquito mesh.
Water
Rainwater harvesting, a sewage treatment plant and two days of storage.
Power
Full generator backup and an EV charging point at every car park.
